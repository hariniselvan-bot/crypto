Our Story

Built by traders, for traders

Founded in 2021, Stackly started with a simple frustration: crypto platforms were either too complex for beginners or too limited for professionals. So we built both — one platform with a beginner-friendly surface and institutional-grade power underneath.

Today, over 2 million users across 180+ countries trust Stackly to buy, sell, stake and secure their digital assets, with more than $18.5 billion in lifetime trading volume.
